Switchback Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Saeed Aghdam handles information in the Switchback Android app.

Information on your device

Switchback saves your selected trails, session history, progress, session feedback, and reminder settings on your device. Optional check-ins can include information about sleep, food, stress or mood, and recovery habits. The app uses these entries to show your progress, plan your next session, and update the widgets and reminders you choose to use.

When you import a compatible file or paste a plan into My trails, Switchback reads the content you select and stores a local copy of the plan and any included exercise pictures. Switchback has no account system, cloud sync, advertising SDK, or analytics service. The app does not upload your rehab logs or imported trails to the developer.

Android and Google Play services

Android handles file selection, notifications, and home-screen widgets. If you use spoken cues, Switchback sends the cue text to your device’s text-to-speech engine. If you open a source link or choose a file from a cloud provider, the browser or provider handles that action under its own privacy policy.

The app includes a connection to Google Play Billing for the optional support feature. Google Play handles availability and purchase status checks. If tips are enabled in a future release and you choose to buy one, Google Play handles payment and sends the app a purchase result. Switchback does not receive your card details. The app can save a supporter record on your device.

Keeping and deleting information

Switchback keeps app data on your device until you clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This removes locally stored progress and imported copies of your plans and pictures. The original files in Downloads or another provider are managed separately.

If you email support, Saeed Aghdam receives your email address and the information you choose to send. It is used to respond to your request and kept as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations. Please omit medical records or other sensitive information when contacting support. You can use the same email address to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence.
